The basics

What sickle cell actually is

Sickle cell disorder is an inherited condition affecting haemoglobin, the part of your red blood cells that carries oxygen. Instead of staying round and flexible, cells can turn stiff and crescent-shaped, getting stuck in small blood vessels.

Know your trait

One blood test. Answers for life.

Carrying sickle cell trait does not make you ill, but if your partner carries it too, it changes what you'd want to know before starting a family. Testing is free on the NHS: ask your GP for a haemoglobinopathy screen.

Privacy notice

Who we are

This website, Sickle Cell Sense, is run by Kwapong Health CIC, a Community Interest Company (company number 17271036). We are responsible for the personal data described here.

Browsing this website

We'd like to use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they use, but it only runs if you say yes to the cookie banner shown on your first visit; it is off by default. If you accept, Google Analytics sets a cookie and collects your IP address (truncated), device and browser type, and the pages you visit; Google's own privacy policy explains how it uses this data. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie preferences" in the footer of any page. Fonts and the rest of the site's code are self-hosted regardless of your choice: Google Analytics is the only script ever loaded from another company, and only once you've agreed to it. Like any website, our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs, such as your IP address and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Donations

When you choose an amount and press the donate button, you leave this website and go to a payment page run by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details, name and email address. We never see or store your card number.

Stripe tells us your name, email address, the amount, the date and whether the gift is monthly. We use this to record and thank you for your donation and to keep our accounts. We keep financial records for six years, as the law requires. Stripe also uses your data for its own purposes, such as preventing fraud, and explains this in its privacy policy. Stripe may process data outside the UK, with safeguards in place.

Donations to a Community Interest Company are not eligible for Gift Aid, so we do not collect the details Gift Aid needs.

If you choose to pay by bank transfer instead, you leave this website and go to a payment page run by NatWest, which pays the money directly into our bank account. NatWest and your own bank handle that payment; we do not see your bank account details.

If you email us

We use your email address and what you tell us only to reply and to deal with your message. We keep it for as long as we need to do that, and then delete it. Please think before sending health details by email. If you choose to, we will treat them as sensitive and use them only to respond to you.

Why we are allowed to use your data

To process a donation we rely on contract, and on our legal duty to keep accounts. To answer your emails and keep the site secure we rely on our legitimate interests, which do not override your rights. We do not sell your data, and we will not use it for marketing unless you have clearly asked us to.

Who we share it with

Only the people who help us run the work: Stripe and NatWest for payments, our email and hosting providers, and our accountants. We may also share data where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it. Get in touch with Kwapong Health CIC and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
